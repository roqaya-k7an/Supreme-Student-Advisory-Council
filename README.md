## 📖 About

The Supreme Student Advisory Council represents and serves the student body of
the International Islamic University, Islamabad. This website is the Council's
home online — a single place for students to:

- Discover who the Council is and what it does.
- Register and join Council activities.
- Explore and sign up for competitions and events.

The site is fully **bilingual**, offering a seamless experience in both
**English** and **Arabic (العربية)** with full right-to-left (RTL) support.
